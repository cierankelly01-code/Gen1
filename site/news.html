News

What's new at Genex.

New machines, new accreditations and new people. Follow us on LinkedIn for the latest.

August 2026

Achilles Verified, and safe edges in a single hit

We've achieved Achilles Verified Supplier status, independent confirmation of our approach to quality, compliance, health and safety and continuous improvement.

The same month, our new hemming tool went into production. It folds a clean, closed safety edge into a panel in one hit, which removes the sharp edge, stiffens the panel and cuts out a deburring operation.

June 2026

A new 5-axis CNC machining centre

Our Hurco 5-axis machine handles more complex geometry with greater precision, and shortens lead times on machined parts.

April 2026

Amada HRB1003 press brake installed

Folding up to 6 mm thick at 3 metres long, with offline programming, automatic crowning and digital inspection. Our team trained with Amada UK on the offline software before it went live.

April 2026

Growing the team

A new Business Development Manager joined to look after existing customers and introduce Genex to new ones, and our team was out meeting customers at MACH 2026 and the Commercial Vehicle Show.

February 2026

ISO 9001 renewed with zero non-conformances

Our annual BSI audit found not a single non-conformance. Doughnuts, cookies, tea and coffee all round.
