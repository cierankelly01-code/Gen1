In short: This glossary explains the terms used in metal pressing, sheet metal fabrication, welding, finishing and supplier quality, from progression tools and hemmed edges to PPAP, PPM and OTIF, in plain English.

A–Z

Terms

Progression tool
A press tool with several stations that a coil-fed strip moves through one step per stroke, producing a finished part on every stroke. Also called a progressive die.
Single-action tool
A press tool that does one operation per stroke, such as blanking or forming.
Multi-stage tooling
A set of separate tools, with the part moved between them for each operation.
Blank
The flat piece of metal cut to shape before it's formed or folded.
Pierce
Punching a hole in sheet metal in a press tool.
Press brake
A machine that folds sheet metal between a punch and a die. Genex's Amada HRB1003 folds up to 6 mm at 3 m.
Crowning
Compensation on a press brake that keeps the bend angle constant along the full length of a long bend.
Springback
The tendency of metal to open up slightly after bending; tools and programs allow for it.
Hem (hemmed edge)
An edge folded back on itself to remove the sharp edge and stiffen the panel.
Bend relief
A small cut where a bend meets an edge, to stop the material tearing.
PEM insert
A self-clinching nut, stud or standoff pressed permanently into sheet metal.
Projection welding
A resistance weld that joins a nut or stud to a sheet through small raised projections.
Spot welding
A resistance weld joining overlapping sheets at points, without filler metal.
MIG welding
Arc welding with a continuously fed wire, used manually or by robot.
TIG welding
Arc welding with a tungsten electrode, used for clean, precise welds, especially on stainless and aluminium.
5-axis machining
CNC machining where the cutter can approach the part from many angles, so complex shapes are made in one set-up.
E-coat
Electrophoretic coating: an even, thin paint layer applied by electrical charge, common on automotive brackets.
Zinc-nickel plating
A plated coating that gives steel higher corrosion resistance than plain zinc.
Powder coating
A dry paint applied electrostatically and cured with heat, giving a thick, hard-wearing coloured finish.
Passivation
A treatment that restores stainless steel's natural corrosion resistance after fabrication.
Pre-galvanised steel
Steel coated with zinc before it's formed, common for construction brackets.
First-article inspection (FAI)
A full check of the first parts against the drawing before production runs.
PPAP
Production Part Approval Process: the automotive industry's evidence pack that a supplier's process makes good parts consistently. Levels 1 to 5 set how much is submitted.
APQP
Advanced Product Quality Planning: the structured way automotive launches are planned.
Control plan
The document that sets which features are checked, how and how often during production.
PPM
Parts per million: a defect rate. Genex reports 0 PPM.
OTIF
On time, in full: the share of orders delivered complete and on schedule. Genex reports 98%+.
ISO 9001
The international standard for quality management systems.
ISO 14001
The international standard for environmental management systems.
IATF 16949
The automotive industry's quality management standard. Genex is working towards it.
Cyber Essentials
A UK government-backed scheme that certifies basic cyber security controls.
Golden thread
The construction industry's requirement for accurate, traceable information about a building and its components.
