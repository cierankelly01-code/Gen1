In short: Genex recruits apprentices and skilled press setters, welders, fabricators, machinists and quality inspectors. Some of the team have been with Genex for over 40 years, and former apprentices have come back to build their careers here.

01 · Why us

Why work at Genex

  • New kit to learn on: an Amada press brake with offline programming, a Hurco 5-axis machining centre and robotic welding, all added recently.
  • People stay: our longest-serving inspector retired in 2026 after more than 40 years.
  • Family values: independent since 2001, with a team culture that extends beyond the shop floor.
