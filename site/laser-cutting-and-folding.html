Laser cutting & folding

Cut to 25 mm. Folded to 6 mm at 3 metres.

Laser cutting and CNC folding for prototypes, low to medium volumes and parts too large or thick for a press, with hemmed safety edges in a single hit.

In short: Genex laser cuts sheet and plate up to 25 mm and folds up to 6 mm thick at 3 metres long on an Amada HRB1003 press brake, programmed offline from your 3D model, with automatic crowning, digital angle inspection and hemming.

01 · Capacity

HemmingClosed safety edges in a single hit
MaterialsMild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, copper, brass

02 · Investment

The Amada HRB1003

Installed in April 2026. Programming offline means the bend sequence is worked out on screen before the sheet reaches the machine, so set-up time drops and the first part is right. Automatic crowning keeps the angle true along the full 3 metres.

03 · Safe edges

Hemmed edges in one hit

A hemmed edge folds the sheet back on itself: no sharp edge, a stiffer panel, and no deburring operation. Read more in hemmed edges explained.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What's the thickest you can laser cut?

Up to 25 mm, depending on the material.

How long and thick can you fold?

Up to 6 mm thick at 3 metres long on our Amada HRB1003.

Do I need tooling for laser cut and folded parts?

No. Laser cutting and folding works from your drawing, which makes it the usual route for prototypes and lower volumes.

Can you fold copper and brass?

Yes, as well as mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium.

What file should I send?

A 3D model (STEP) is best, because we program the press brake offline from it. A dimensioned PDF or DXF works too.
