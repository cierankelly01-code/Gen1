Industries

Pressings and assemblies for automotive, aerospace, defence and industry

Most of our work is for automotive Tier 1 suppliers. We also take vehicle start-ups from prototype to production, and make pressings and fabrications for construction, medical, airport-security and machinery customers.

Our core market

Automotive & EV

We supply Tier 1 interior, chassis and EV-systems suppliers with structural brackets, reinforcements, console structures and thermal-system brackets for programmes running in the UK and Europe. We work to OEM material and coating standards and submit PPAP to Level 3 as standard.

  • Centre-console and interior structure brackets
  • EV heater, pump and thermal-management brackets
  • Fuel, filter and under-body mounting brackets with weld nuts
  • E-coated and zinc-plated brackets to OEM corrosion specs

Examples: a fuel filter bracket, an aluminium door card bracket and a welded stainless exhaust hanger.

EV and vehicle start-ups

Most new vehicle programmes start with a handful of prototypes and an uncertain volume. We work the same way you do:

  • Laser-cut and folded prototypes with no tooling cost
  • Tooling quoted as a separate decision, so you only commit when the design is frozen
  • Engineering changes after the tool is built quoted before any work starts
  • PPAP only if your customer needs it. Otherwise first-article inspection

Industrial, construction and consumer

Brackets, fixings, plates, enclosures and fabricated steelwork, at the volumes and unit costs these markets need, with galvanised, zinc and powder-coated finishes managed for you.

  • Construction: cable basket ceiling brackets and raised-floor base plates for data centres and offices
  • Medical: mortuary preparation tables in mirror-polished 304 stainless
  • Airport security: access panels for scanner lanes
  • Machinery: lawn mower rear rollers, rolled and welded from sheet

Aerospace, defence or regulated medical work? Tell us the quality standard and documentation you need, and we'll tell you straight whether we're the right fit.

Parts we make

Eight parts, with the material and process route for each

Customer names and part numbers are left out for confidentiality. Where a part is e-coated or powder coated, the coating is done by our approved finishing partners and managed by us.

AutomotivePressing with welded studs

Fuel filter bracket

Holds two high-pressure fuel pumps and fixes to the vehicle at three points. It is a two-part assembly, so that if a pump ever fails, it fails safe.

Material
S355MC, 1.5 mm
Added parts
M6 weld studs
Finish
Black e-coat, by our approved coating partner
  1. 01Single-action press, 100 t
  2. 02Projection weld, 75 kVA
  3. 03E-coat (sub-contracted)
  4. 04Final inspect and pack

Engineering detail: Pierced holes held to within 0.15 mm.

AutomotiveLarge aluminium pressing

Door card bracket

Part of a door card assembly. It lets parts be mounted to the door behind the final trim covering. Roughly 350 mm wide by 350 mm high.

Material
Aluminium, 1.5 mm
Added parts
M6 clinch nuts
Size
About 350 × 350 mm
  1. 01Single-action press, 100 t
  2. 02Clinch nut insertion (PEM press)
  3. 03Final inspect and pack

Engineering detail: The tooling had to stop the aluminium crumpling and give a uniform finish across a large, flat part.

AutomotiveWelded assembly

Exhaust hanger

Two pressed components welded together with two wire forms. The bracket wraps around the exhaust and hangs from the vehicle on rubber mounts.

Material
Stainless steel, 1.5 mm
Added parts
M6 clinch nuts, 2 wire forms
  1. 01Single-action press, 100 t
  2. 02Robotic weld
  3. 03Clinch nut insert (rivet gun)
  4. 04Inspect and pack

Engineering detail: Tight tolerance on the wire-form positions after welding, and weld quality checked by macro penetration measurement.
