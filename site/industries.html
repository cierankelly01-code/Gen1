Industries

Pressings and assemblies for automotive, aerospace, defence and industry

Most of our work is for automotive Tier 1 suppliers. We also take vehicle start-ups from prototype to production, and make pressings for industrial customers.

Our core market

Automotive & EV

We supply Tier 1 interior, chassis and EV-systems suppliers with structural brackets, reinforcements, console structures and thermal-system brackets for programmes running in the UK and Europe. We work to OEM material and coating standards and submit PPAP to Level 3 as standard.

  • Centre-console and interior structure brackets
  • EV heater, pump and thermal-management brackets
  • Fuel, filter and under-body mounting brackets with weld nuts
  • E-coated and zinc-plated brackets to OEM corrosion specs

EV and vehicle start-ups

Most new vehicle programmes start with a handful of prototypes and an uncertain volume. We work the same way you do:

  • Laser-cut and folded prototypes with no tooling cost
  • Tooling quoted as a separate decision, so you only commit when the design is frozen
  • Engineering changes after the tool is built quoted before any work starts
  • PPAP only if your customer needs it. Otherwise first-article inspection
