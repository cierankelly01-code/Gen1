In short

Genex designs progressive, compound and single-stage stamping dies to your press data, builds and tries them out in Walsall, UK, and ships them with sample parts and a dimensional report. Die build typically takes 12–17 weeks from frozen data, and the quote states the total time to your dock.

From purchase order to running at your plant

  1. Die concept and quote

    Strip layout, station count, die size and weight, plus a delivered-to-dock timeline.

  2. Design approval

    Die design signed off against your press data and die standard before any steel is cut.

  3. Build

    Typically 12–17 weeks from order and frozen part data.

  4. Try-out and sampling in Walsall

    The die runs in our press. You receive sample parts and a dimensional report, and can witness try-out in person or by video.

  5. Ship

    Crated for export, sea or air freight to your dock.

  6. Install and run-off

    Remote or on-site support at your plant, as agreed in the quote.

Die standards and components

We build to your die standard if you have one. Otherwise we propose ours and you approve it at design stage. Units (metric or inch), component brands and spare-part availability in your country are agreed before the build, so your maintenance team can source wear parts locally.

You own the die once it's paid for. Ask us about supply of die design files with the tool.

Try-out evidence before it ships

  • Sample parts from the finished die
  • Dimensional report against your drawing
  • Try-out witnessed in person or by live video
  • Spares package and wear-parts list
  • Die drawings and setup sheet

Shipping, duty and installation

Dies ship crated for export by sea or air. We quote EXW as standard, or FCA, DAP or DDP on request, and provide the commercial invoice, certificate of origin and packing list your customs broker needs.

Duty depends on the tariff classification and current US rules for steel content, which have changed several times. We'll give your broker the classification data and origin information, and you decide the Incoterm that suits you.

Questions

Can a UK die shop build a die to run in a US press shop?

Yes. We design to your press data, build and try out in Walsall, send sample parts and a dimensional report, then ship the die crated to your plant. Die standard, components and units are agreed at quotation.

How long does it take?

Die build typically takes 12–17 weeks from order and frozen data, plus try-out, sampling and freight. The quote states the total time to your dock.

Can you also supply the stampings until our press is free?

Yes. We can run the die in Walsall and ship parts while you prepare to take it over, then ship the die.
