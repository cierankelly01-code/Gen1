Launch management

  1. Feasibility & DFM

    Material, tolerance and process risks are raised at quotation, not at try-out.

  2. Design & FMEA

    Tool design and PFMEA are reviewed together, with your team invited.

  3. Try-out & sampling

    First-off samples come with a dimensional report, so issues are closed before PPAP.

  4. Run @ rate & PSW

    Capacity is proven at line rate before sign-off.
