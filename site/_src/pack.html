Quality, launch & commercial terms

From RFQ to PPAP

  1. RFQ review & DFM: an engineer replies within one working day
  2. Quotation: piece price, tooling broken down tool by tool, lead times and assumptions
  3. Tool design approval: strip layout and design signed off with you
  4. Build, try-out & sampling: first-off parts with dimensional report
  5. PPAP / PSW: Level 3 as standard, about 4 weeks after tooling
  6. Serial production: to your call-offs, packed to your spec
