In short: A Genex project runs in seven stages: enquiry, manufacturability review, quotation, first parts (inspection report or PPAP), production with in-process checks, finishing and assembly, then packing and delivery on Genex's own vehicles. Every stage is recorded against the order, so it's traceable from enquiry to delivery.

01 · Stages

The seven stages

  1. Enquiry

    You send a drawing or 3D model, quantities, material and finish. We reply within one working day. NDA on request.

  2. Manufacturability review

    We check the design against the route: features that add cost, material availability, tolerances that need special attention. Problems are raised now, not at first-off.

  3. Quotation

    A written quote with the process route, piece price, any tooling quoted separately, and lead times. Where pressing and laser cut and fold are close, we price both.

  4. First parts

    A first-article inspection report as standard, or a PPAP submission to the level your customer requires.

  5. Production

    Parts made to your schedule, with first-off, in-process and final inspection against the control plan, and material certificates held against every batch.

  6. Finishing and assembly

    Coatings managed through approved partners, inserts fitted, assemblies built and tested, all under one part number.

  7. Packing and delivery

    Packed to your specification, labelled for your goods-in, and delivered on our own 3.5 t or 7.5 t vehicles.

03 · Problems

If something goes wrong

Call us first, so we can contain it straight away. Problems are logged and investigated under our ISO 9001 system, with corrective action owned by a named person and dated containment. Anyone can also raise a quality or environmental concern directly with our Managing Director through our concern form.
