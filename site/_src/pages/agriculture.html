Plant

Plant on site

Pressing and welding are on the same site in Walsall. Production tools are maintained in our own toolroom, so a worn punch or a breakdown is dealt with on site.

Presswork and welding at Genex
PressesTwelve mechanical presses, 40 to 200 tonnes, with coil feed for progression tools
Material on the pressesUp to 5 mm
Robotic weldingFanuc robotic MIG arm
Manual weldingThermal Arc MIG and Jasic TIG stations
Resistance weldingFour British Federal resistance welders, 75 to 150 kVA, for projection, spot, stud and nut welding
ToolroomEight toolroom machines on site, so press tools are maintained and repaired in-house

Materials

Materials and finishes

Coated steels, such as pre-galvanised, suit outdoor parts; cut edges and welds may need touching up. Mild steel usually needs protection: e-coat, zinc or zinc-nickel plating, or powder coat. E-coat gives an even, thin corrosion-resistant coat that reaches into recesses.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you make parts for agricultural equipment?

Yes. Rollers, brackets, guards and welded assemblies for agricultural and off-highway equipment, in coated steels.

How are parts protected against corrosion?

Mild steel usually needs protection: e-coat, zinc or zinc-nickel plating, or powder coat, applied by our approved finishing partners and managed by us.

What volumes do you press?

From a few hundred to hundreds of thousands of parts a year.
