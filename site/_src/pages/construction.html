  5. Delivery

    Packed to your specification and delivered on our own vehicles.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do you make for construction?

High-volume brackets and fixings, such as cable basket ceiling and wall brackets, plus prefabricated frames and modular assemblies.

Can you support the golden thread?

Yes. Every order is traceable, with material certificates and records from enquiry to delivery.

Which steels suit outdoor and site use?

Pre-galvanised and coated steels are common for construction brackets; we can advise on the finish for your environment.
