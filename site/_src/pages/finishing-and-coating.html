03 · Materials

Which finish for which material

  • Mild steel usually needs protection: e-coat, zinc or zinc-nickel plating, or powder coat.
  • Stainless steel is often left bare, polished or passivated.
  • Aluminium can be left bare or powder coated.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you do the coating yourselves?

Coatings are applied by our approved, audited finishing partners and managed by us, so you deal with one supplier.

Which finish is best for automotive brackets?

E-coat and zinc-nickel are common on automotive brackets.

Do stainless steel parts need a finish?

Often not, but passivation after fabrication helps stainless resist corrosion, and polishing gives a cosmetic finish.
