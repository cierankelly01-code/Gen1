01 · Fasteners

02 · Process

How they're fitted

Nuts and studs can be inserted in the press tool, or as a separate operation in our PEM and projection welding cells. We also fit mechanical clinch fasteners and rivnuts, and weld studs on a dedicated stud welder.

Plant on site
Resistance weldingFour British Federal resistance welders, 75 to 150 kVA, for projection, spot, stud and nut welding
Stud weldingA dedicated stud welder

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which fasteners can you fit?

PEM nuts, studs and standoffs, clinch nuts, rivnuts, weld nuts and weld studs.

Can nuts and studs be fitted in the press tool?

Yes. Nuts and studs can be inserted in the press tool, or as a separate operation in our PEM and projection welding cells.

What is a PEM insert?

A self-clinching nut, stud or standoff pressed permanently into sheet metal.
