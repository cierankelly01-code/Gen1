In short

Genex UK supplies metal pressings, welded assemblies and press tools to manufacturers outside the UK. We quote in GBP, EUR or USD on EXW to DDP terms and prepare export documentation in-house.

Two ways we supply international programmes

Model A

Parts, shipped

We build the tool, run production in Walsall and ship finished, packed parts to your plant or 3PL on your Incoterms.

Model B

Dies, proven then shipped

We design and build the die to your press data, prove it on our presses with sample parts, then ship it to run in your own press shop. How it works →

Tool build for export

Every exported tool is tried out and sampled on our own presses first. You receive dimensional reports, sample parts and tool drawings before it leaves Walsall, so the tool arrives ready to run, not ready to debug.

  • Tool design to your press data: shut height, bolster, feed line
  • Spares package and wear-parts list included
  • Remote or on-site installation support

Questions international buyers ask

Can a UK supplier make pressings for a US or European programme?

Yes. We quote ex works or on agreed Incoterms, in GBP, EUR or USD, and prepare export documentation including commercial invoices, certificates of origin and material certificates.

Can you build a tool here and ship it to our plant?

Yes. Tools are proven out on our presses with samples and a dimensional report, then shipped to run in your press shop.

How do you handle time zones?

UK hours overlap eight of the nine hours of the central-European working day and the morning in US Eastern time.
