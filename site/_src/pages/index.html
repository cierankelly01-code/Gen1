    Buyer FAQ

    Questions buyers ask before a first order

    Or speak to our team on 01922 710050.

    What do you make?

    Metal pressings, laser-cut and folded parts, welded fabrications, machined parts, bent tube and complete assemblies. Most of our work is mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium, from one-off prototypes to repeat runs of millions.

    What thickness can you work with?

    Pressings up to 5 mm on presses up to 200 tonnes. Folding up to 6 mm at 3 metres long on our Amada press brake. Laser cutting up to 25 mm, depending on the material.

    Can you supply a finished assembly instead of separate parts?

    Yes, and it's often where we save customers the most. We bring together pressed, folded, welded and machined parts with inserts, bought-in components and finishing, then assemble, inspect and pack the finished product under one part number.

    What do you need to quote?

    A drawing or 3D model (STEP, DXF or PDF), the material, how many you need per order or per year, any finish, and when you need them. If you have an approval or packaging requirement, send that too.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes. We run our own 3.5 tonne and 7.5 tonne vehicles, with racking and crates made to protect your parts, and we can label and document deliveries to suit your goods-in.

    Which standards do you hold?

    ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and Cyber Essentials, and we are an Achilles Verified Supplier. We are working towards IATF 16949. Download the certificates.
