In short: Genex turns pressed, folded, welded and machined parts into finished products: PEM, stud and nut inserting, tube manipulation, coatings managed through approved partners, assembly and customer-specified testing, then inspection, packing and delivery on its own 3.5 t and 7.5 t vehicles, under one part number.

01 · Build

One part number instead of many

When presswork, welding, fabrication, finishing and assembly are coordinated through one manufacturing partner, you buy, inspect and receive one thing. That means less supplier administration, less assembly work on your line and clearer accountability.

02 · Scope

What we bring together

  • Our own pressed, laser-cut, folded, welded and machined parts
  • Fasteners: PEM nuts, studs and standoffs, clinch nuts and weld nuts
  • Bought-in components: fittings, plastics, rubber mounts, hardware
  • Coatings and finishes through approved partners
  • Customer-specified tests and checks
  • Packing, labelling and documentation to your specification

03 · Inserting

Inserting fasteners

PEM nuts, studs and standoffs, clinch nuts and weld nuts fitted by us, so fasteners arrive in the part rather than loose in a bag, and your line doesn't need to fit them.

04 · Tube

Tube manipulation

Bent tube frames and wire-form assemblies in stainless and mild steel, welded to pressings and brackets where the design needs it.

05 · Protection

Finishing, managed by us

E-coat, zinc and zinc-nickel plating, powder coating, passivation and more, through our approved, audited finishing partners. We manage the logistics, the specification and the inspection, so your parts arrive finished on one order. Not sure which finish? See metal finishes explained.

Quality

How every part is checked

  • First-off inspection against the drawing before a batch runs
  • In-process checks against the control plan while it runs
  • Final inspection before packing, with material certificates held against the batch
  • Traceability from enquiry to delivery, with customer-specific reports if you need them
  • PPAP Levels 1 to 5 when your customer requires it

Certified to ISO 9001 and ISO 14001, and re-audited by BSI in 2026 with zero non-conformances. More on quality.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you supply a finished assembly?

Yes. We bring our own parts together with inserts, bought-in components and finishing, then assemble, inspect and pack the finished product under one part number.

Do you do the coating yourselves?

Coatings are applied by our approved, audited finishing partners and managed by us, so you deal with one supplier.

Do you deliver?

Yes, on our own 3.5 tonne and 7.5 tonne vehicles, with racking and crates to protect your parts.

Can you source bought-in parts?

Yes. Send the bill of materials and we'll source, check and fit them as part of the assembly.
