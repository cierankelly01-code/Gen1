Plant

Plant on site

Pressing and welding happen on the same site in Walsall, so pressed parts don't have to go to another supplier to be welded.

Presswork and welding at Genex
PressesTwelve mechanical presses, 40 to 200 tonnes, with coil feed for progression tools
Material on the pressesUp to 5 mm
Robotic weldingFanuc robotic MIG arm
Manual weldingThermal Arc MIG and Jasic TIG stations
Resistance weldingFour British Federal resistance welders, 75 to 150 kVA, for projection, spot, stud and nut welding
InspectionMitutoyo CNC coordinate measuring machine, 500 × 700 × 400 mm, checking parts back to the 3D CAD model

Materials

Materials and finishes

Mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium. Robotic MIG suits repeatable seam welds on steel assemblies; TIG suits stainless, aluminium and cosmetic welds. Coatings are applied by our approved finishing partners and managed by us: see finishing and coating.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do you offer rail suppliers?

Pressings and welded assemblies (robotic MIG, manual MIG and TIG), with full finishing, packed complete.

How is weld quality checked?

Weld quality is checked against your specification. On our robot-welded exhaust hangers, for example, penetration is measured by macro section.

Can you supply assemblies finished?

Yes. Coatings are applied by our approved, audited finishing partners and managed by us, so you deal with one supplier.
