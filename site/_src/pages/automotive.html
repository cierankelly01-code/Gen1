Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you supply automotive Tier 1s?

Yes. Automotive has been a core sector for decades, including parts for BMW and Land Rover programmes.

Are you IATF 16949 certified?

We're working towards IATF 16949. Today our automotive work runs under ISO 9001 with the AIAG core tools: APQP, PPAP, FMEA, MSA and SPC.

Can you submit PPAP?

Yes, Levels 1 to 5, as your customer requires.

Which coatings do automotive parts get?

Commonly e-coat or zinc-nickel to the OEM specification, applied by our approved finishing partners and managed by us.
