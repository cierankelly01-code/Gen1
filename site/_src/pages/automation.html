Materials

Materials and finishes

Mild steel suits brackets, panels and frames: lowest cost, easy to form and weld. It needs a coating against corrosion: e-coat, zinc or zinc-nickel plating, or powder coat. Powder coating gives a coloured, hard-wearing finish for visible parts. See metal finishes explained.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do you make for automation?

Conveyor frames, machine guards, safety gates, fabricated bases and support structures.

Which weld process suits frames and guards?

Manual MIG suits fabrications, frames, guards and larger parts, from one-off to medium volumes.

Can guards have safe edges?

Yes. A hemmed edge folds the sheet back on itself: no sharp edge, a stiffer panel, and no deburring operation.
