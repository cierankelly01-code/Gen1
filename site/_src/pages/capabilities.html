01 · Presswork

Metal pressings

Repeatable pressed components at medium to high volume. We run progression tools fed from coil for long runs, and single-action and multi-stage tools for simpler or lower-volume parts. Production tools are maintained in our own toolroom, so a worn punch or a breakdown is dealt with on site, fast.

02 · Sheet & plate

Laser cutting & folding

Laser cutting and CNC press-brake folding for prototypes, low to medium volumes and parts too large or thick for a press. Our Amada HRB1003, installed in 2026, folds up to 6 mm thick at 3 metres long. It's programmed offline, so set-up time drops and the first part is right, and automatic crowning keeps the bend angle true along the full length.

Cutting & folding
Laser cuttingUp to 25 mm, depending on material
FoldingUp to 6 mm thick at 3 m long
ProgrammingOffline, from your 3D model
AccuracyAutomatic crowning, digital angle inspection
HemmingClosed safety edges in a single hit
MaterialsMild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, copper, brass

Why hem an edge? A hemmed edge removes the sharp edge for safety, doubles the material to stiffen the panel (often letting you keep a thinner gauge), and replaces a deburring or linishing operation.
