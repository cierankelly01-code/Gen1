A real part

The cable basket ceiling bracket

Hangs cable basket from the ceiling for electrical installations in buildings. A wall-mount version is made too, and both come in different lengths to suit the job. Pre-galvanised steel, 3 mm, and a high-volume production part.

The route:

  1. Single-action press, 100 t
  2. Inspect and pack

Plant

Plant on site

Brackets can run on the presses at high volume. We also laser cut and fold copper and brass.

Material on the pressesUp to 5 mm
VolumesA few hundred to hundreds of thousands of parts a year
ToolroomEight toolroom machines on site, so press tools are maintained and repaired in-house

Materials

Materials and finishes

Pre-galvanised steel is coated before forming, so this bracket goes straight from the press to inspection and packing. Cut edges and welds may need touching up. We laser cut and fold copper and brass for electrical and decorative parts.
