02 · Quality

Checked, not assumed

Weld quality is checked against your specification. On our robot-welded exhaust hangers, for example, penetration is measured by macro section and wire-form positions are held to a tight tolerance after welding.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you have robotic welding?

Yes. Robotic MIG is used for repeatable, high-volume welded assemblies.

Can you TIG weld stainless steel?

Yes. Manual TIG is used for stainless and cosmetic work, such as our polished stainless mortuary tables.

Can you weld nuts and studs to pressings?

Yes, by projection welding in jigged cells, checked to your torque or push-out requirement.

Do you weld aluminium?

Yes, with manual MIG and TIG.
