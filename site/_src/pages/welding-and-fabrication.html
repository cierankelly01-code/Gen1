03 · Quality

Checked, not assumed

Weld quality is checked against your specification. On our robot-welded exhaust hangers, for example, penetration is measured by macro section and wire-form positions are held to a tight tolerance after welding. Weld nuts and studs are checked to your torque or push-out requirement.

04 · Design

Design tips for welded assemblies

  • Leave room for the torch or electrode to reach every weld
  • Show weld type and size with standard weld symbols on the drawing
  • Tell us which faces are cosmetic, so welds and spatter stay off them
  • For volume work, design parts to locate in a jig: it's what makes robotic welding repeatable
  • Long or heavy welds on thin sheet can distort it: talk to us early about sequence and fixturing

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you have robotic welding?

Yes. Robotic MIG is used for repeatable, high-volume welded assemblies.

Can you TIG weld stainless steel?

Yes. Manual TIG is used for stainless and cosmetic work, such as our polished stainless mortuary tables.

Can you weld nuts and studs to pressings?

Yes, by projection welding in jigged cells, checked to your torque or push-out requirement.

Do you weld aluminium?

Yes, with manual MIG and TIG.

Can you supply welded assemblies finished and painted?

Yes. Coatings are managed through our approved finishing partners, so the assembly arrives finished.
