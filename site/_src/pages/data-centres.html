03 · Process

How a data centres project runs with Genex

  1. Enquiry and review

    Send the drawing, volumes and specification; we review manufacturability and flag cost risks.

  2. Quote

    Route, piece price, any tooling and lead time, in writing.

  3. First parts

    First-article inspection report, or PPAP to the level required.

  4. Production

    Parts made to your schedule, with in-process and final checks.

  5. Delivery

    Packed to your specification and delivered on our own vehicles.
