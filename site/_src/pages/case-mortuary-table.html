In short: Genex made a stainless steel mortuary preparation table as a complete product: laser-cut and press-brake folded stainless, TIG welded and mirror polished, with the sink, taps and drain sourced and fitted, so the customer received a finished table ready to install.

01 · The part

Mortuary preparation table

A purpose-built table for body preparation and cleaning in a mortuary. It needs a hygienic, easy-to-clean surface, a sink for cleaning, and a drain set in to remove waste fluids.

02 · Route

How we make it

  1. 01Laser cut from stainless sheet
  2. 02Folded on the press brake
  3. 03TIG welded
  4. 04Mirror polished
  5. 05Sink, taps and drain sourced and fitted
  6. 06Inspected, packed and delivered

03 · Detail

The engineering detail

Designed and built complete as a dedicated product. TIG welding keeps the welds clean on stainless, and the mirror polish leaves a surface that's easy to keep hygienic.

04 · Result

What the customer gets

One finished, ready-to-install product, instead of separate metalwork, plumbing parts and a fitting job: one order, one delivery and one point of accountability.
