02 · Policies

Our policies

Quality Policy

Genex UK Ltd specialises in the production of metal pressings and welded subassemblies for the automotive and wider manufacturing industries. Quality is fundamental to Genex’s reputation, customer trust, competitiveness and long-term success. We are committed to providing products and services that consistently meet or exceed customer expectations and all applicable statutory and regulatory requirements by understanding customer needs, controlling our processes, preventing defects and delivering reliably.

Genex maintains a Quality Management System aligned with ISO 9001. We set and review measurable quality objectives, monitor performance, address risks and opportunities, develop our people, and use audits, customer feedback, non-conformance data and management review to drive continual improvement. Every employee has a responsibility for quality and is encouraged to identify and support improvements that strengthen our products, services and business performance.

Environmental Policy

Genex UK Ltd recognises that protecting and improving the environment is essential to responsible and sustainable manufacturing, and to the future of our business, employees, customers and community. We are committed to minimising the environmental impacts of our activities, preventing pollution, using energy and resources efficiently, reducing scrap and general waste, and seeking practical opportunities to improve the environment through better working practices and investment.

Genex maintains an Environmental Management System aligned with ISO 14001. We identify environmental aspects and compliance obligations, set and review measurable environmental objectives, monitor energy use, waste and emissions, and use audits, performance data and management review to improve the effectiveness of the system. We will fulfil applicable legal and other requirements, engage employees and suppliers, and consider environmental protection in our business decisions as part of our commitment to continual improvement.

04 · Traceability

Every order, traceable from enquiry to delivery

From the moment your enquiry arrives, through purchasing, manufacture, finishing and delivery, every stage is recorded against your order. Give us one number and we can tell you where the material came from, how the part was made and when it shipped.

  • Material certificates held against every batch
  • First-off, in-process and final inspection against the control plan
  • Customer-specific reports and container tickets to speed up your goods-in
  • Corrective action with a named owner and dated containment

05 · Measurement

Measurement and inspection equipment

Dimensional results come from our own Mitutoyo CNC coordinate measuring machine (CMM), with a 500 × 700 × 400 mm measuring volume, checking parts back to the 3D CAD model rather than a hand-measured sample.

06 · First parts

First-article inspection and PPAP

New parts are approved before production starts: a first-article inspection report as standard, or a full Production Part Approval Process (PPAP) submission when your customer needs one. Our processes and controls are set up so you get the right part, first time, every time.

07 · Environment

Environmental management

Our ISO 14001 system covers how we use energy and materials, manage waste and work with our finishing partners. We have been optimising our processes against rising energy costs for over a decade, and eco-conscious coatings and materials are available on request. Construction customers can rely on us for the traceability the golden thread requires.

08 · Next step

Working towards IATF 16949

With support from the Walsall High Growth Programme, we are investing in the systems and training to gain IATF 16949, the automotive industry's quality management standard. Until then, our automotive work runs under ISO 9001 with the AIAG core tools (APQP, PPAP, FMEA, MSA and SPC) your customers expect.
