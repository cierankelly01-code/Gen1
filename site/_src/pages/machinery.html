A real part

A lawn mower rear roller, from flat sheet

The roller at the back of a lawn mower. We start from flat steel sheet, roll it and weld it to shape. Mild steel, 1.5 mm, e-coated by our approved coating partner. It is one real example of our rolled and welded work.

The route:

  1. Roll
  2. Seam weld
  3. MIG weld
  4. Hydraulic press

Materials

Materials and finishes

Mild steel is the lowest-cost choice for frames, brackets and panels, and needs a coating against corrosion. Stainless steel resists corrosion and suits food use: it is often left bare, polished or passivated. See materials.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do you make for machine builders?

Machine frames, guards, brackets, panels and sub-assemblies, including for food-processing and packaging equipment.

Can you weld stainless for food equipment?

Yes. TIG welding suits stainless, aluminium, cosmetic and food-safe welds, and stainless can be polished or passivated.

Do you only make one-offs, or volume too?

Both. Laser cutting and folding run from one-off prototypes to repeat batches, and pressing takes over when volumes justify a tool.
