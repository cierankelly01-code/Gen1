Quality

How every part is checked

  • First-off inspection against the drawing before a batch runs
  • In-process checks against the control plan while it runs
  • Final inspection before packing, with material certificates held against the batch
  • Traceability from enquiry to delivery, with customer-specific reports if you need them
  • PPAP Levels 1 to 5 when your customer requires it

Certified to ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. BSI's 2026 audit of our ISO 9001 system found zero non-conformances. More on quality.

What to send for a quote

  • A 3D model (STEP) and a dimensioned drawing (PDF or DXF)
  • Material and thickness, and any equivalent grades you'd accept
  • Quantity per order and per year
  • Finish or coating, and any cosmetic faces
  • Critical tolerances, and the approval you need (first-article report or PPAP level)
  • When you need the first parts
  • Tube diameter, wall thickness and bend radii

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which materials do you work in?

Tube frames and assemblies are made in stainless and mild steel.

Can you weld tube to other parts?

Yes. Tube frames are welded to pressings and brackets where the design needs it.

Can you supply the finished assembly?

Yes. We bring our own parts together with inserts, bought-in components and finishing, then assemble, inspect and pack the finished product under one part number.
