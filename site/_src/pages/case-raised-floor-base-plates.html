In short: Genex presses 3 mm mild steel base plates for raised-floor systems used in data centres and offices on a 100 tonne progression press, so each part is made complete in a single coil-fed pass at the lowest price per part.

01 · The part

Raised-floor base plates

Base plates form part of an assembly that raises the floor so services can run underneath the working area. Typical uses are data centres and offices, where floors are laid in large areas and parts are needed in volume.

The engineering detail
