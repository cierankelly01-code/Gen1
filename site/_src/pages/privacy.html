Privacy policy

Who we are

Genex UK Ltd (company no. 04157489), Fryers Road, Leamore Industrial Estate, Walsall WS2 7NA, is the controller of personal data collected through this website.

What we collect

Contact details and files you send through our quote form or by email. We don't use cookies. We count visits to this site with our own statistics tool, which stores nothing on your device and does not identify you. It records the page viewed, the site you came from, your screen size, your language and your approximate location, which is worked out from your IP address; the address itself is not kept. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, your visit is not counted.

How we use it

We use your details to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations and manage customer relationships, on the basis of our legitimate interest in running our business or to take steps before entering a contract.

Concern reports

If you raise a concern through our website, what you send, including any photos or documents you attach, goes to our quality team and is used only to investigate and respond. You can report anonymously, in which case we do not collect your name or contact details.

Your drawings

Drawings and CAD files are treated as confidential, used only to quote and manufacture, and never shared outside Genex and our approved finishing partners without your consent.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by emailing info@genexuk.co.uk. You can also complain to the ICO.
