A real part

Scanner-lane access panels, step by step

Made in a range of sizes to finish the framework of security scanner lanes in airports. Mild steel, 1.5 mm, with an undercoat and powder topcoat by our approved coating partner.

The route:

  1. Laser cut
  2. Press brake
  3. MIG weld
  4. Polish
  5. CD stud weld
  6. Undercoat and powder topcoat, by our approved coating partner

Plant

Plant on site

The panels are cut, folded and welded, then coated by our approved coating partner. We manage the coating, so they arrive finished on one order.

Sheet-metal fabrication at Genex
Laser cuttingUp to 25 mm, depending on material
FoldingAmada HRB1003 press brake: up to 6 mm thick at 3 m long, programmed offline from your 3D model
Manual weldingThermal Arc MIG and Jasic TIG stations
Stud weldingA dedicated stud welder

Materials

Materials and finishes

Mild steel for the access panels, with an undercoat and powder topcoat. Powder coating gives a thicker, coloured, hard-wearing finish for visible parts. We also cut and fold stainless steel and aluminium.
