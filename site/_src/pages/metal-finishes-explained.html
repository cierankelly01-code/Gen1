In short: E-coat gives an even, thin corrosion-resistant coat that reaches into recesses, and is common on automotive brackets. Zinc and zinc-nickel plating protect steel sacrificially. Powder coating gives a thicker, coloured, hard-wearing finish for visible parts. Passivation helps stainless steel resist corrosion. Genex manages all of these through approved finishing partners.

Common finishes compared

E-coatEven, thin corrosion protection that reaches into recesses; automotive brackets and under-body partsUsually black; often a base for further coats

02

How to choose

Start from where the part lives and what the drawing or your customer's specification requires. Tell us the environment, any colour or appearance requirement, and any threads or fits that must stay clear, and we'll recommend a finish and manage it for you.

One supplier, finished parts

Finishes are applied by our approved, audited partners. We manage the logistics, the specification and the inspection, so your parts arrive finished on one order. See assembly and finishing.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Genex apply coatings in-house?

Coatings are applied by our approved, audited finishing partners and managed by us, so you deal with one supplier.

Which finish is best for automotive brackets?

E-coat and zinc-nickel are common on automotive brackets. Follow the OEM or Tier 1 specification on your drawing.

Do stainless steel parts need a finish?

Often not, but passivation after fabrication helps stainless resist corrosion, and polishing gives a cosmetic finish.
