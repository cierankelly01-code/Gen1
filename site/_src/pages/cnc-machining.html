03 · Route

When machining is the right route

  • Features that need tighter tolerances than pressing or folding can hold
  • Thick sections and solid parts that can't be formed from sheet
  • Complex 3D shapes, pockets and threads
  • Low volumes of parts that would otherwise need a dedicated tool

04 · Design

Design tips for machined parts

  • Use standard thread sizes and hole diameters where you can
  • Avoid deep, narrow pockets and sharp internal corners: cutters are round
  • Only tolerance the features that need it; everything else to a general tolerance
  • Tell us how the part locates in its assembly, so datums match its function

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What machining do you offer?

5-axis CNC milling on a Hurco machining centre, plus turning and grinding.

Can you machine features on pressed parts?

Yes. Holes, threads and faces that need tighter tolerances than a press can hold are finished by machining.

Can you supply machined parts on their own?

Yes, as well as machined features within a larger assembly.

Why 5-axis?

It machines complex geometry in a single set-up, which improves accuracy and shortens lead times.
