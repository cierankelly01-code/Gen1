Questions

Frequently asked questions

What machining do you offer?

5-axis CNC milling on a Hurco machining centre, plus turning and grinding.

Can you machine features on pressed parts?

Yes. Holes, threads and faces that need tighter tolerances than a press can hold are finished by machining.

Can you supply machined parts on their own?

Yes, as well as machined features within a larger assembly.
