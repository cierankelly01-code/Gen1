In short

Genex is a UK press tool maker and metal pressings manufacturer. We design and build tools in-house, run them at 5,000 to 250,000+ parts a year in steels up to 3.0 mm, and submit PPAP to Level 3. Tooling typically takes 12 to 17 weeks, and up to 20 for multi-tool programmes.

Toolroom

Tool design & build

Every tool starts with a strip layout and a simulation of how the part will form. Our toolmakers design for the press the tool will actually run on, matching shut height, tonnage, feed pitch and coil width. Every tool is tried out and sampled before PPAP, and run at rate before sign-off.

  • Progressive tools for high-volume brackets and clips, with pierce, notch, form and cut-off in one stroke.
  • Compound and single-stage tools for lower volumes, deep features or large blanks.
  • Checking fixtures and weld jigs designed together with the tool, so inspection and fit match the datums.

Press shop

Production pressings at medium to high volume

Once approved, your tool goes straight into production in the same building. We run coil-fed progressive work and blank-fed stage tooling for brackets, reinforcements, clips, plates and covers in mild steel, high-strength low-alloy steel, stainless and aluminium.

Tools are maintained on a stroke-count schedule, and in-process checks to the control plan catch dimensional drift early.

Launch

From RFQ to PPAP, step by step

  1. RFQ review and DFM · day 1

    An engineer reviews your STEP and drawing and flags material, tolerance or feature risks before quoting.

  2. Quotation · days 3–5

    Piece price, tooling cost broken down tool by tool, lead times and assumptions, all in writing.

  3. Tool design approval · weeks 1–4

    Strip layout and tool design are signed off with you before any steel is cut.

  4. Build and try-out · to week 12–17

    Tool build, try-out and first-off samples with a dimensional report.

  5. PPAP / PSW · +4 weeks

    Full submission to the level you require, Level 3 as standard, including capability studies.

  6. Serial production

    Parts ship on your call-off schedule from PPAP approval.

Tooling stage payments: 30% on order · 30% on design approval · 30% on tool completion · 10% on PPAP submission.
