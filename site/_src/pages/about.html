About Genex

Making pressings in Walsall since the 1980s, family-owned since 2001.

Genex started inside larger engineering groups in the 1980s. A management buyout in 2001 made it independent, and it has been family-owned since.

Our story

Genex UK's history goes back to the 1980s, when the business operated under the ownership of larger organisations. In 2001 a management buyout made Genex an independent, family-run company.

Today the business runs its own toolroom, press shop, laser, welding and assembly on one site, supplying automotive Tier 1s, vehicle start-ups and industrial customers.
