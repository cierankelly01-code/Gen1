The people behind the parts

Engineering experience. Family business values.

Genex has made parts in Walsall since 1980. A management buyout in 2001 made it independent, and it has been family-run ever since.

Our story

Genex UK's history goes back to 1980, when the business operated under the ownership of larger organisations. In 2001 a management buyout made Genex an independent, family-run company.

Since then we have grown steadily while staying true to those family values. The experience and commitment of our team have driven more capacity, more capabilities and deeper technical knowledge, and some of our people have been with us for over 40 years.

Looking ahead, we are focused on structured, sustainable growth: continued investment in new markets and capabilities, while keeping the family culture that defines who we are and how we work.

Investment

New capability, every year.

We put money back into machines, systems and people, so our customers can bring us more of their product.

How we work

We build more than parts. We build partnerships.

Quality without compromise

Zero defects per million and zero audit non-conformances are the standard we hold ourselves to, not a target.

One partner, fewer hand-offs

Pressing, folding, welding, machining, finishing and assembly from one supplier means less administration for you and clear accountability from us.

Straight answers

If a design will be expensive to make or a material will cause trouble, we tell you at the quote, along with what we'd do instead.
