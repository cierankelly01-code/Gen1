02 · Process

From your model to a finished part

  1. Your model

    We work from your STEP file or drawing and check it for manufacturability.

  2. Programmed offline

    The bend sequence is worked out on screen before any metal is cut, so problems are solved before set-up.

  3. Laser cut

    The flat blank is cut to profile, with holes and features.

  4. Folded

    The Amada folds each bend, with crowning keeping the angle true along the length.

  5. Checked

    Angles are inspected digitally and the part is checked against the drawing.

03 · Investment

Installed in April 2026, after our team trained on the offline programming with Amada UK. Programming offline means set-up time drops and the first part is right. Automatic crowning keeps the angle true along the full 3 metres, and digital inspection confirms it.

04 · Safe edges

Hemmed edges in one hit

A hemmed edge folds the sheet back on itself: no sharp edge, a stiffer panel, and no deburring operation. Read more in hemmed edges explained.

05 · Design

Design tips for folded parts

  • Keep flanges at least 4× the material thickness long, so the press brake can grip them
  • Add bend relief where a bend meets an edge, to stop tearing
  • Keep holes and slots clear of bends (at least 2× thickness plus the bend radius)
  • Use the same inside bend radius throughout where you can: fewer tool changes, lower cost
  • Allow extra flat length for any hemmed edge
  • Send a STEP file: we program the folding straight from it

Starting guidelines, not limits. See the design guide.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What's the thickest you can laser cut?

Up to 25 mm, depending on the material.

How long and thick can you fold?

Up to 6 mm thick at 3 metres long on our Amada HRB1003.

Do I need tooling for laser cut and folded parts?

No. Laser cutting and folding works from your drawing, which makes it the usual route for prototypes and lower volumes.

Can you fold copper and brass?

Yes, as well as mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium.

What file should I send?

A 3D model (STEP) is best, because we program the press brake offline from it. A dimensioned PDF or DXF works too.

Can parts longer than 3 metres be made?

Folding is limited to 3 metres, but longer parts can often be fabricated from sections. Send the drawing and we'll advise.
