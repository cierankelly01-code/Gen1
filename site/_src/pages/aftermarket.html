TubeBent tube frames and assemblies in stainless and mild steel

Laser cuttingUp to 25 mm, depending on material
FoldingAmada HRB1003 press brake: up to 6 mm thick at 3 m long, programmed offline from your 3D model
Safe edgesHemmed edges closed in a single hit: no sharp edge, a stiffer panel

Materials

Materials and finishes

Stainless steel is often left bare, polished or passivated, and polishing gives a cosmetic finish. Aluminium can be left bare or powder coated. Powder coating gives a coloured, hard-wearing finish for visible parts.
