Welding & fabrication

The right weld for the part.

Robotic MIG for repeatable volume, skilled manual MIG and TIG for complex or cosmetic welds, and spot and projection welding for sheet assemblies and weld nuts.

In short: Genex welds fabrications and assemblies with robotic MIG, manual MIG and TIG, spot and projection welding, in mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium, from one-off parts to jigged high-volume work, alongside presswork, folding and assembly on the same site.

01 · Processes

Four weld processes under one roof

Having the right process available lets engineers design for performance, quality and cost, not for the limits of one supplier's equipment.

Robotic

Robotic MIG

Consistent, repeatable welds across high-volume assemblies.

Manual

MIG & TIG

Complex, specialist and cosmetic welds, including stainless.

Resistance

Spot welding

Sheet-to-sheet joints for brackets and housings.

Resistance

Projection welding

Weld nuts and studs, jigged and checked.

02 · Quality

Checked, not assumed

Weld quality is checked against your specification. On our robot-welded exhaust hangers, for example, penetration is measured by macro section and wire-form positions are held to a tight tolerance after welding.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you have robotic welding?

Yes. Robotic MIG is used for repeatable, high-volume welded assemblies.

Can you TIG weld stainless steel?

Yes. Manual TIG is used for stainless and cosmetic work, such as our polished stainless mortuary tables.

Can you weld nuts and studs to pressings?

Yes, by projection welding in jigged cells, checked to your torque or push-out requirement.

Do you weld aluminium?

Yes, with manual MIG and TIG.
