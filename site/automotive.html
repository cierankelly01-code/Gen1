Automotive pressings and assemblies, to PPAP.

In short: Genex supplies automotive OEMs and Tier 1s with pressed brackets, reinforcements and welded sub-assemblies in aluminium, stainless and coated steels, with PPAP to the level the customer requires, robotic welding for repeatability, a 0 PPM defect record, and IATF 16949 in progress.

01 · Products

What we make for automotive

  • Brackets and reinforcements
  • Robot-welded sub-assemblies such as exhaust hangers
  • Large aluminium pressings such as door card brackets
  • Fuel-system and under-body brackets with weld studs
  • E-coated and zinc-nickel plated parts to OEM coating specifications

02 · Requirements

What you need from a supplier, and how we meet it

Approval

PPAP, Levels 1 to 5

First-article inspection as standard; full PPAP to the level your customer asks for.

Quality

0 PPM

Our defect record, under ISO 9001, working towards IATF 16949.

Repeatability

Robotic welding

Consistent welds across high-volume assemblies.

Supply

03 · Process

How a automotive project runs with Genex

  1. Feasibility

    Drawing, volumes and OEM specifications reviewed; material and process risks raised at quote stage.

  2. Quote

    Piece price, tooling and timing, quoted separately so you can compare.

  3. Launch

    Tooling arranged, first-off samples with a dimensional report.

  4. PPAP

    Submission to the level your customer requires, with control plan and capability evidence.

  5. Serial supply

    Parts to your call-offs, packed to line-side specification, with full traceability.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you supply automotive Tier 1s?

Yes. Automotive has been a core sector for decades, including parts for BMW and Land Rover programmes.

Are you IATF 16949 certified?

We're working towards IATF 16949. Today our automotive work runs under ISO 9001 with the AIAG core tools: APQP, PPAP, FMEA, MSA and SPC.

Can you submit PPAP?

Yes, Levels 1 to 5, as your customer requires.

Which coatings do automotive parts get?

Commonly e-coat or zinc-nickel to the OEM specification, applied by our approved finishing partners and managed by us.
