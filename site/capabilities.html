01 · Presswork

Metal pressings

Repeatable pressed components at medium to high volume. We run progression tools fed from coil for long runs, and single-action and multi-stage tools for simpler or lower-volume parts. Production tools are maintained in our own toolroom, so a worn punch or a breakdown is dealt with on site, fast.

Presswork
Press capacityMechanical presses up to 200 tonnes
Tool types runProgression (coil-fed), single action, multi-stage
Material thicknessUp to 5 mm
MaterialsMild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, coated steels
VolumesA few hundred to millions of parts
In-press operationsPierce, form, nut and stud insertion

02 · Sheet & plate

Laser cutting & folding

Laser cutting and CNC press-brake folding for prototypes, low to medium volumes and parts too large or thick for a press. Our Amada HRB1003, installed in 2026, folds up to 6 mm thick at 3 metres long. It's programmed offline, so set-up time drops and the first part is right, and automatic crowning keeps the bend angle true along the full length.

Cutting & folding
Laser cuttingUp to 25 mm, depending on material
FoldingUp to 6 mm thick at 3 m long
ProgrammingOffline, from your 3D model
AccuracyAutomatic crowning, digital angle inspection
HemmingClosed safety edges in a single hit
MaterialsMild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, copper, brass

Why hem an edge? A hemmed edge removes the sharp edge for safety, doubles the material to stiffen the panel (often letting you keep a thinner gauge), and replaces a deburring or linishing operation.

03 · Joining

Welding & fabrication

The right weld process for the part, not just the one a supplier happens to own. Robotic MIG for repeatable volume work, skilled manual MIG and TIG for complex or cosmetic welds, and spot and projection welding for sheet assemblies and weld nuts.

05 · Build

Inserting & complete assembly

A finished assembly is worth more than a box of parts. We bring together our own pressed, folded, welded and machined parts with PEM fasteners, studs and nuts, bought-in components and customer-specified testing, then pack the finished product ready for your line or for installation. You get one part number, one inspection record and one delivery.

More on assembly and finishing →

06 · Protection

Finishing & coating

Environmental protection and cosmetic finishes through our approved, managed supply partners: e-coat, zinc and zinc-nickel plating, powder coating, passivation and more. We manage the logistics, the coating specification and the inspection, so you deal with one supplier.

07 · Tube

Tube manipulation

Bent tube frames and wire-form assemblies in stainless and mild steel, welded to pressings and brackets where the design needs it. Our classic-car boot racks, made in stainless and aluminium, started here.
