Sheet & plate

Laser cut & fold

Fibre laser cutting and CNC press-brake folding for prototypes and low to medium volumes. It's how you get parts while your production tool is being built.

Laser & folding envelope
Cutting thicknessUp to 25 mm (material-dependent)
MaterialsMild steel, stainless, aluminium, galvanised
FoldingCNC press brake, multi-axis back gauge
Typical leadDays, not weeks, for prototype quantities

Precision

Machining

Turning, milling and grinding, used every day to build and maintain our own press tools and available for bespoke components and post-press machining. Holes, threads and faces that need tighter tolerance than a press can hold are finished here.

Joining

Welding

Small-batch and fully jigged high-volume welding. Projection welding of M5–M8 nuts and studs to pressings, plus spot, MIG and TIG welding for fabricated sub-assemblies.

Projection

Weld nuts & studs

Jigged, repeatable and checked to your torque or push-out spec.

Sheet-to-sheet assemblies for brackets and housings.

MIG & TIG

Build

Assembly

We bring the complete product together: pressings, fasteners, bought-in components and finishing, then pack it ready for dispatch or for fitment at your line. You get one part number, one inspection report and one delivery.

Protection

Finishing

Environmental protection and cosmetic finishes through our approved, audited supply partners: e-coat, zinc and zinc-nickel plating, powder coating and passivation. We manage the logistics, the coating spec and the inspection, so you deal with one supplier.
