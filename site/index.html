    Buyer FAQ

    Questions buyers ask before a first order

    Speak to our engineering team on 01922 710050.

    Do you design and build press tools in-house?

    Yes. Our toolroom designs and builds progressive, compound and single-stage tools, plus checking fixtures and welding jigs. Production then runs on the same site, so one supplier owns the tool, the part and the PPAP.

    What are typical lead times?

    Tooling typically takes 12 to 17 weeks from order and frozen data, and up to 20 weeks for multi-tool programmes. PPAP submission follows in about 4 weeks, then serial production on your call-offs. Laser and fabricated work can ship in days.

    What do you need to quote?

    A STEP file or drawing, material spec, annual volume and finish (see our RFQ checklist). If you have a PPAP level or packaging spec, send that too. An engineer reviews every RFQ, replies within one working day and flags anything that will make tooling expensive.

    Who owns the tooling, and how is it paid?

    You do, once it's paid for. Our standard stage payments are 30% on order, 30% on design approval, 30% on tool completion and 10% on PPAP submission. We maintain and store your tools at no charge while they're in production.

    Will you take over tooling from another supplier?

    Yes. We assess transferred tools on arrival, refurbish them if needed and re-validate them on our presses. This is a common way for customers to move a failing source without re-tooling.

    Do you supply outside the UK?

    Yes. We quote ex works or on agreed Incoterms, in GBP, EUR or USD, and handle export documentation in-house. See Global supply.
